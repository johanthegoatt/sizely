Europe sizes children by height in centimetres; the US and UK go by age. Height is the one fixed number, so it's the safest to go by.

One thing worth knowing

Sizing is not standardised. Two brands with the same label can be a size apart, which is why fit, not price, is the top reason clothes and shoes get sent back. So treat a converted size as a starting point, and where you can, go by the measurement: foot length for shoes, chest, bust or waist for clothes. That is the one number that does not change between countries.
